# Requirements

AUTH-01 registration/login/logout; WORK-01 isolated home/facility workspaces; MEDIA-01 private validated picture/video/text upload and playback; AI-01 asynchronous source-grounded general operations drafts with honest provider failures; SOP-01 draft/review/approval/revision/export; JOURNAL-01 persistent records; TEAM-01 membership and roles; TRAIN-01 assigned approved version and acknowledgement; AUDIT-01 attributable append-only application audit; UX-01 responsive keyboard-accessible interface.

## Learning framework extension

LESSON-01 source analysis creates procedure and lesson drafts atomically. LESSON-02 publish only from an approved procedure and freeze lesson content. LESSON-03 private persisted step progress and reflection questions. DOC-01 private PDF/TXT/MD alongside picture/video uploads. CAMERA-01 phone/browser USB camera capture with permission and lifecycle cleanup. GUIDE-01 explicitly started sampled visual observations against an approved general-operations step, with uncertainty and bounded usage. CHAT-01 private workspace-scoped reactive text assistance; VOICE-01 optional browser dictation and read-aloud replies; PROACTIVE-01 record-derived reminders. PROVIDER-01 server-only OpenAI/Anthropic adapters with honest funding failures. DESIGN-02 dark Midnight Fieldwork design system and responsive implementation.

Framework limitations: no guaranteed real-time mistake detection, native scanner drivers, continuous realtime voice/video, automatic scoring, autonomous record mutations or background push notifications. Live model quality and physical device support require funded/provider and device validation.

COLLECTION-01 independent plant/seed/material profiles; COLLECTION-02 dated notes and private photo evidence; COLLECTION-03 user-entered measurement history and photo comparison; ISSUE-01 manual open/resolved status with permissions and version checks; DESIGN-03 cannabis-inspired identity while retaining the accepted dark layout. These records do not generate lessons or alter lesson progress. Automated cultivation guidance, diagnosis and growth forecasts are excluded.

COLLECTION-04 integrate management into My collection, not a separate garden navigation item. AREA-01 named 1–12 row/column layouts linked to existing profiles, with move/remove and occupancy protection. TASK-01 user-authored record/area deadlines, snooze, completion/cancellation and reopening. ALERT-01 app-wide due/restock alerts, optional open-app browser notifications and downloadable calendar alarms. STOCK-01 user-entered material quantities, audited signed adjustments and nonnegative stock. STOCK-02 threshold alerts and restock quantities derived from user targets. All management records are shared within their workspace and independent of lessons.

SITE-01 multiple named physical sites with descriptive locations and notes. PROJECT-01 grow projects spanning one or more workspace sites, optional dates and planned/active/paused/completed lifecycle. PROJECT-02 aggregate record/area/material counts and task completion. SCOPE-01 assign and filter collection records, layouts, tasks and materials by site/project. SCOPE-02 preserve legacy unassigned records, enforce tenant references, reject stale assignment writes and explicitly conflicting linked scopes. Site/project groups use existing workspace permissions.
